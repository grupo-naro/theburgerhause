# Combos (contenido fijo) Implementation Plan

> Executed inline (owner opted out of the design questions: "directo"). Decisions below are mine, stated to the owner.

**Goal:** A product can be a combo: it has its own price (the discount is a lower price) and a fixed list of contents (`4 × Holtber simple + 2 × Coca 500`). Each sale keeps what the combo contained. The kitchen ticket lists the contents, the drinks inside a combo come off the stock, and Analíticas counts its patties.

**Decisions (mine):**
- **Contents are fixed.** The customer does not choose what goes inside; that is a possible phase 2.
- **No nesting:** a combo cannot contain another combo, and it cannot contain itself.
- **Quantity:** 1 to 20 per component.
- **Contents are edited** on the product edit page, section "Contenido del combo", by admin and encargado, like the rest of the product.
- **Price:** the admin form shows "Por separado: $X · Ahorro: $Y". The combo price stays the product price.
- **Each sale snapshots its contents** in `order_items.combo_snapshot` (jsonb `[{ name, quantity }]`).
- **Patties and cost:**
  - Patties snapshot = the combo product's own `patties` if > 0, otherwise Σ(component patties × quantity).
  - Cost snapshot = the combo's own `cost` if set, otherwise Σ(component cost × quantity) when every component has a cost, otherwise NULL.
- **Stock:** the combo units are expanded into their components inside `reserveStockForOrder` and `adjustStockForOrder`, so the callers are untouched. Returns already work from the order's movements.
- **Availability:** a combo is unavailable when any component is inactive, paused by stock, or in an inactive category. It is hidden from the public menu and the POS, and `resolveUnits` rejects it with `PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE`.
- **Shown in:**
  - the kitchen ticket and the customer ticket, as sub-lines `· 4x Holtber simple`;
  - the product sheet, as `Incluye: 4 × Holtber simple, 2 × Coca 500`;
  - the dispatch card item summary.

## Tasks

1. **Schema + admin core + admin section.**
   - The `combo_items` table and the `order_items.combo_snapshot` column, in migration `0020_combos`.
   - `src/lib/menu/combos.ts`: `getComboItems`, `setComboItems` (validation: no self, no nesting, unknown products refused, quantities 1–20, atomic replace) and `comboSeparatePrice`.
   - `saveComboItemsAction` (manager) plus its `surface.ts` entry.
   - The product edit page section.
   - Tests: int tests for the core and the action, plus e2e.
2. **Sale.** The catalog loads the combo components. `resolveUnits` handles availability. `insertOrderItems` writes the snapshots (contents, patties, cost). Stock reservation and adjustment expand the combos. Tests: int (create order, counter, add-on, stock).
3. **Display.**
   - `PrintableItem.comboItems`;
   - the kitchen and customer tickets;
   - the public menu (`includes`, hiding unavailable combos) and the product sheet;
   - the dispatch summary.
   - Tests: unit tests for the tickets, int for the menu, e2e for the store and the kitchen ticket.

**Gates per task:** `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm test:int`, `pnpm test:security` when the surface changes, `pnpm build` at the end, and the affected e2e specs. Then a final whole-branch review.
